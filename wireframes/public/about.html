About us

A small studio with a long attention span.

We started appardas to prove that software teams can be senior, honest, and calm — and still move fast.

Our story

appardas began with a clear purpose: to bridge the digital divide for Small and Medium Enterprises without the excessive overhead, bloated timelines, or confusing technical jargon common in traditional agencies.

Under the watchful guidance of our feline executive board, we partner with a select group of businesses at a time. We listen to your story, design distinctive digital experiences, and build robust automated tools that help local enterprises stand proud in the modern market.
