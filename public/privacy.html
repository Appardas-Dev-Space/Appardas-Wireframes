Privacy Notice

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Plain-language summary: We collect the minimum we need. Staff control exactly which work details appear publicly. You can ask us to correct or delete your information at any time.

1. Who we are

appardas ("we", "us") operates this website. For questions about this notice or your data, contact privacy@appardas.example.

2. What we collect and why

DataPurposeBasis
Inquiry details (name, email, company, message)Respond to your requestConsent / legitimate interest
Staff public profile dataIdentify our team and build trustConsent
Basic analytics (aggregated)Improve the websiteLegitimate interest

3. Staff public profiles

Public team profiles show only work-related information that each individual has explicitly approved. Optional fields (work email, desk phone, city, professional links) are hidden unless the person opts in. Personal contact details, employment records, compensation, and identity documents are never published. Staff can request edits or removal at any time, and profiles are retired promptly when someone leaves.

4. Retention

Inquiries are retained for up to 24 months, then deleted. Staff profile data is retained only while published and is removed when consent is withdrawn or employment ends.

5. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of your data, and you may object to processing. Contact us and we'll respond within the period required by applicable law.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies for security and functionality. Optional analytics cookies are only set with your consent.

7. Changes

We'll update this page when our practices change and revise the "last updated" date above.
